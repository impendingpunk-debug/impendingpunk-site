02 Belleview, Florida · late ’90s

The perfect crime.

FRIDAY NIGHT
↳
a game or two

One of my favorite old memories is riding with my brother, Jason, and our dad to the Blockbuster that had just opened in Belleview, Florida.

It was the weekend, which meant family movies and—if we were lucky—a game or two.

But Jason and I already knew what we wanted.

Dad probably knew what we were going to try to get, too.

GoldenEye 007.

By then, GoldenEye had already been out for a couple of years. But we hadn't really had a rental place nearby before that Blockbuster opened.

The games we had played up until then were games Mom had actually bought for us to own.

Zelda.Mario Kart.

Games that sat next to the Nintendo and belonged to us.

But I wanted GoldenEye.

Jason wanted GoldenEye.

There was only one problem.

We were way too young to be playing it.

So, naturally, we came up with a plan.

Jason grabbed another game. Something completely random.

Then we put it on top of GoldenEye.

The perfect crime.

I carried both games up to Dad, apparently believing that if GoldenEye was underneath another box, a grown man would somehow be unable to see it.

Dad looked at the games.

And rented both of them.

At the time, Jason and I probably thought we'd pulled off one of the greatest schemes in history.

Now that I'm a dad myself, I'm pretty sure Dad knew exactly what we were doing.

Either way, we got GoldenEye.

We went home and spent that weekend playing a Rated M game years before either of us had any business playing it.

And more than twenty-five years later, it's still one of my most cherished memories.

Not just because GoldenEye was a great game.
